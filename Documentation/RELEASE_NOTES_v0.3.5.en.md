# Cozy Survivors v0.3.5 — All Content Unlocked

Release date: September 11, 2026

Android version code: 8

- All animals, regions, endless modes, village decorations and letters are available from the start.
- Fixed content becoming locked in non-development Android builds, including Google Play app bundles.
- Previous Unlock all OFF settings no longer restrict access, and the OFF toggle has been removed.
- Existing progress, achievements, stars and saved adventures are preserved. Combat, leveling and rewards follow the existing rules.
